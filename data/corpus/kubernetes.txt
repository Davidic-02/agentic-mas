Kubernetes health probes

Kubernetes supports exactly 3 probe types: liveness, readiness and startup.
A container may define at most one probe of each type. All three answer a
process-level question -- is this container up and able to accept traffic.

None of them can express whether a workload produced a correct result. For
ordinary services that distinction rarely matters, because a service that
returns the wrong answer is usually a bug rather than an expected mode of
operation.
